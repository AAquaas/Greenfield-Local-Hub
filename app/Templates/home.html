Welcome to Greenfield Local Hub!

The Greenfield Local Hub (GLH) is a cooperative of local farmers and food producers. Each item of organic food produced is harvested and collected using eco-friendly methods. We provide quality items at affordable prices so anyone here can enjoy the fresh taste of our products without burning a hole in your wallet.



Why Buy Locally?

Buying locally means you can get your favourite products like steak and milk fresher as well as it being better for the environment due to all of our producers being local, so there is minimal transport time to our store!
